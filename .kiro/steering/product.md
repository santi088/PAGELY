# Product context

This is an **Android-only .NET MAUI app**: an offline PDF/EPUB reader plus an online
Letterboxd-style layer where users search books, write reviews, and comment.

## Current mission: UI/UX polish ONLY
- The app already works. Features and logic are FROZEN.
- Goal: make it look and feel like a modern, polished Android reading app
  (calm, bookish, fast, readable), without changing what it does.
- Target: Android only. Ignore iOS, Windows, and Mac Catalyst concerns. Do not add
  platform branching for them.

## Design personality
- Calm and content-first: books and covers are the heroes, chrome stays quiet.
- Reading screens are distraction-free; social screens are warm and lively.
- Consistent across every screen: same spacing, radii, type scale, and colors.
